Twin-Deck Yacht No.2

Twin decks, private cabin and plenty of shaded space on board.

About This Boat

Twin-Deck Yacht No.2 features twin decks and a private cabin with toilet, galley and a kettle for hot water. A crew of two to three assists with snorkelling, fishing and other activities. For the full-day trip we recommend no more than four stops; if you choose more, some may be trimmed to fit the overall schedule. Rates exclude crew gratuity (suggested USD 10–30 per boat). The deposit can be paid online; the balance is settled in cash on the day of your trip.

Key Features

  • ✓ Twin decks
  • ✓ Private air-conditioned cabin and toilet
  • ✓ Snorkelling gear (mask, snorkel, life jacket) / fishing equipment (mainly handline)
  • ✓ Light Arabic-style lunch
  • ✓ Snorkelling and fishing guidance

Sample Itinerary

  • 09:00 · Boarding at the marina

    We set sail once marina security clears us, heading for the nearshore coral reserves.

  • 09:40 · Snorkelling & swimming

    We drop anchor at the designated snorkelling spot, with gear and guidance provided.

  • 11:30 · Sea fishing

    We anchor at the fishing grounds, tackle and guidance provided.

  • 13:00 · Lunch on board

    Lunch is served on the boat, with free time to unwind.

  • 14:30 · Island visit

    Stroll an unspoilt island — fine sand and crystal-clear water.

  • 16:00 · Return to marina

    Back to the marina, ending a lovely day at sea (start times can be adjusted).
