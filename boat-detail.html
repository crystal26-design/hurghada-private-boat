Twin-Deck Yacht No.1

Twin decks, sunbathing cushions and a private cabin — ideal for family days out and small gatherings of friends.

About This Boat

Twin-Deck Yacht No.1 offers a spacious double-deck layout with cabin, toilet and galley, and comes with a crew of around three — skipper, cook and snorkelling safety guide. For the full-day trip we recommend no more than four stops; if you choose more, some may be trimmed to fit the overall schedule. Rates exclude gratuity (suggested USD 10–30 per boat). The deposit can be paid online; the balance is settled in cash on the day of your trip.

Key Features

  • ✓ Twin sun decks with cushioned lounge seating
  • ✓ Private cabin and toilet
  • ✓ Snorkelling gear / fishing equipment
  • ✓ Light Arabic-style lunch, soft drinks and mineral water

Sample Itinerary (illustrative only — the actual plan may change with your requests and sea conditions on the day)

  • 09:00 · Boarding at the marina

    We set sail once marina security clears us, heading for the shallow coral reefs.

  • 09:40 · Snorkelling & swimming

    We drop anchor at the designated snorkelling spot, with gear and guidance provided.

  • 11:30 · Sea fishing

    We anchor at the fishing grounds, tackle and guidance provided.

  • 13:00 · Lunch on board

    Lunch is served on the boat, with free time to unwind.

  • 14:30 · Island visit

    Stroll an unspoilt island — fine sand and crystal-clear water.

  • 16:00 · Return to marina

    Back to the marina, ending a lovely day at sea (start times can be adjusted).
