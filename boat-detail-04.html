Classic Glass-Bottom Yacht

Sea fishing and snorkelling on board — watch the fish swim beneath your feet through the glass-bottom hull.

About This Boat

This classic glass-bottom boat features a shaded cabin, WC and a transparent glass bottom. A crew of around 2 takes care of navigation, fishing guidance and snorkelling safety.
Not included: crew gratuity (suggested: USD 10–30 per boat).
Dolphin sightings are possible but not guaranteed.
Deposit can be paid online (WeChat / Alipay / UnionPay); the balance is settled in cash on the day of the trip.

Sample Itinerary (indicative only — actual route may vary depending on your wishes and sea conditions on the day)

  • 09:00 · Boarding at the Marina

    We set sail for the fishing grounds once the marina security clears us.

  • 09:30 · Sea Fishing

    We drop anchor at the fishing spot; gear and guidance provided.

  • 11:30 · Snorkelling & Swimming

  • 13:00 · Return to Marina

    We cruise back to the marina, ending a wonderful day at sea.
