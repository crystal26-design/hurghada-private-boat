Large Yacht

Plenty of space on board — ideal for bigger groups.

About This Yacht

This yacht offers generous open space, a cabin, WC and galley, with 3–5 crew depending on group size handling navigation, cooking and snorkelling safety.
For full-day charters we recommend choosing no more than 4 activities. If too many are selected and the itinerary cannot fit them all, some may have to be dropped on the day.
Not included: crew gratuity (suggested: USD 15–45 per boat).
Deposit can be paid online (WeChat / Alipay / UnionPay); the balance is settled in cash on the day of the trip.

Key Features

  • ✓ Two decks with padded seating
  • ✓ Private cabin & WC
  • ✓ Snorkelling gear / fishing equipment
  • ✓ Arabic-style light meals, soft drinks & mineral water

Sample Itinerary (indicative only — actual route may vary depending on your wishes and sea conditions on the day)

  • 09:00 · Boarding at the Marina

    We set sail for the shallow coral reefs once the marina security clears us.

  • 09:40 · Snorkelling & Swimming

    Anchor down at a designated snorkelling reef, with equipment and guidance on hand.

  • 11:30 · Sea Fishing

    We drop anchor at the fishing spot; gear and guidance provided.

  • 13:00 · Lunch on Board

    Dine on deck and relax at your leisure.

  • 14:30 · Inselstopp

    Stroll a wild little island with powdery sand and crystal-clear turquoise water.

  • 16:00 · Return to Marina

    We cruise back to the marina, ending a wonderful day at sea (start time can be adjusted).
