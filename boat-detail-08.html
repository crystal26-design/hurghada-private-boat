Mid-Size Speedboat

Compact design — nimble, agile and easy to handle.

About This Speedboat

This boat has a retractable sun canopy and usually comes with one captain who handles navigation and snorkelling safety. For the 4-hour trip we recommend choosing no more than 3 activities; if too many are selected and cannot fit into the time available, some may have to be dropped on the day.
The standard dolphin-watching trip suits most guests, but with so many boats around the dolphins you generally cannot swim with them; the dolphin-swim trip requires good swimming ability.
Not included: crew gratuity (suggested: USD 6–30 per boat).
Deposit can be paid online (WeChat / Alipay / UnionPay); the balance is settled in cash on the day of the trip.

Key Features

  • ✓ Sun deck with padded seating
  • ✓ Snorkelling gear / fishing gear (hand lines only)

Sample Itinerary (indicative only — actual route may vary depending on your wishes and sea conditions on the day)

  • 09:00 · Boarding at the Marina

    We set off for the dolphins' favourite waters once the marina security clears us.

  • 09:20 · Dolphin Watching

    With a bit of luck you will see dolphins — sometimes a few, sometimes a whole pod. It always depends on the day.

  • 10:40 · Snorkelling

    Anchor down at the snorkelling spot, with equipment and guidance provided.

  • 12:00 · Island Stop

    Stroll a wild little island with powdery sand and crystal-clear turquoise water.

  • 13:00 · Return to Marina

    We head back to the marina, ending a wonderful day at sea.
