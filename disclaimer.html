1. Platform Nature & Intermediary Statement

This website is an information and intermediary enquiry platform. It only provides intermediary services such as presenting the fleet, suggesting itineraries and connecting you with quotes. The platform itself does not own, operate or skipper any vessel; the actual sea trips are provided by licensed local boat owners / operators.

No contract of carriage, custody or other offline service relationship arises between the platform and the user; the counterparty to any charter agreement is the boat owner / operator, not this platform.

All losses arising from problems with the boat owner's / operator's equipment or on-site service — including personal injury, loss of property, itinerary changes and extra charges — are compensated by the boat owner / operator. This platform bears no joint liability.

Water sports and other outdoor activities carry inherent risks. We strongly recommend taking out suitable insurance before you travel to reduce your losses.

2. Accuracy of Information

We do our best to keep vessel specifications, prices, images and itineraries accurate and up to date. However, due to seasonality, vessel condition, weather and supplier changes, the information is indicative only and does not constitute a commitment. The written confirmation from the boat owner / operator before departure prevails.

If you spot information that is clearly wrong, please let us know via the contact page — we will verify and correct it promptly.

3. Third-Party Services

Sea trips, transfers, photography and extra entertainment options are provided by third parties. The platform bears no responsibility for the quality, safety, punctuality or compliance of third-party services; any related dispute is to be settled directly between the user and the service provider.

We carry out a basic qualification check on partner boat owners, but this check is not a guarantee of their ability to perform.

4. Sea Trips & Personal Safety Risks

Activities at sea involve risks such as waves and swell, collisions, drowning, sunburn, seasickness and contact with marine life. Taking part is taken as an acknowledgement and voluntary acceptance of those risks.

  • Please follow the skipper's and guide's instructions at all times and wear your safety equipment correctly;
  • Minors, elderly guests and non-swimmers must be accompanied and supervised at all times;
  • In the event of bad weather, sea-state warnings or vessel breakdown, the boat owner may cancel or reschedule; the platform will assist but bears no liability.

The platform is not liable for harm caused by the user's own breach of the rules, concealment of health conditions or failure to exercise due care.

5. Health & Fitness to Sail

Pregnant guests, people with cardiovascular conditions, those prone to severe seasickness and others who are not suited to activities at sea should seek medical advice before travelling. Users confirm that the health and group-size details they provide are accurate; any discomfort or accident resulting from concealment is the user's own responsibility.

6. Fees & Payment

This platform does not collect payment online and does not integrate any payment gateway. The website forms are used only to gather trip enquiries; all quotes are subject to a written breakdown confirmed manually by a consultant.

Actual charges are collected by the boat owner / operator, and the payment methods and receipts are those they provide. The platform is not responsible for disputes arising from exchange-rate movements, cross-border fees or third-party payment channels.

7. Booking, Cancellation & Rescheduling

A booking is only confirmed once the boat owner / operator confirms it. Cancellation and rescheduling policies follow the terms of the specific order and usually include:

  • Free rescheduling in case of force majeure such as bad weather or sudden vessel breakdown;
  • Cancellation by the user may incur a pro-rata handling fee;
  • The exact percentages are those set out in the confirmation note.

The platform can help with communication, but does not replace the boat owner's / operator's cancellation policy.

The intellectual property rights in the text, images, logos and layout of this website belong to the platform or its licensors and may not be copied, reproduced or used commercially without written permission. Photos supplied by boat owners are licensed for display on this platform only.

9. Privacy & Personal Data

We collect your name, contact details and travel preferences only to the extent needed to handle your enquiry, so that we can reply with a quote and follow up on the service. We do not sell your personal data.

Your details may be shared with the relevant boat owner / operator to the extent necessary for them to perform the service. You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data via the contact page.

10. External Links

The website may contain links to third-party sites. Their content and privacy practices are their own responsibility, and the platform accepts no liability for external sites.

11. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

The formation, interpretation and dispute resolution of this policy are governed by the law of the relevant jurisdiction. In the event of a dispute, the parties shall first seek an amicable settlement; failing that, the matter shall be referred to the competent court / arbitration body.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us using the details on the Contact & Booking page.
